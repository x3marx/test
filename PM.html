Precious Metals Management Audit

A focused review of governance, custody, accountability, financial treatment, recovery and end-to-end traceability of precious metals associated with manufacturing operations.

Why this audit matters

High-value materials may move across several functions, systems and external parties.
Fragmented procedures may create gaps in accountability, custody, reconciliation and recovery.
Differences in ownership arrangements may affect risk, accounting and settlement responsibilities.
Inaccurate balances, delayed recovery or unsupported write-offs may create financial exposure.
Scope boundary: The review focuses on precious-metal governance and controls. It does not assess catalyst activity, selectivity, production yield or process efficiency.

Audit Objective and Scope

A single statement suitable for the audit plan, engagement memorandum or presentation.

To assess the adequacy, completeness, and implementation of the governance framework and internal controls over the end-to-end management of precious metals, and determine whether they are effectively governed, adequately safeguarded, accurately recorded, valued, reconciled, recovered, settled, and written off throughout their lifecycle in compliance with applicable corporate, contractual, financial, regulatory, and EHSS requirements.

Key Scope Areas

Six high-level categories covering the full control environment without expanding into operational performance.

01

Governance and Compliance

Governance, roles, responsibilities, policies, procedures, management oversight and compliance requirements.

02

Planning and Procurement

Requirements planning, quantity determination, procurement, contracting and ownership arrangements.

03

Physical Control and Custody

Receipt, storage, safeguarding, issuance, use, movement and transfer.

04

Accounting Treatment

Recording, traceability, classification, valuation, reconciliation, adjustments and financial reporting.

05

Recovery and Disposal

Recovery, reclamation, refining, settlement, write-off and disposal.

06

Third-Party Management

Third-party custody, responsibilities, oversight, balance confirmation and contractual compliance.

Key Audit Questions

The principal questions the engagement is expected to answer.

01Is there a complete end-to-end governance framework for precious metals?
02Are responsibilities, ownership and transfers of custody clearly defined?
03Are requirements, quantities and procurement arrangements justified and authorised?
04Are precious metals safeguarded and traceable across locations and custodians?
05Are operational, inventory, third-party and financial records complete and reconciled?
06Are losses, adjustments and write-offs investigated, supported and approved?
07Are recovery, refining and settlement activities adequately controlled?
08Were corrective actions related to previous findings fully and effectively implemented?

Initial Data Required

A focused first request. Detailed supporting evidence can be requested after process walkthroughs and sample selection.

Governance

  • Policies, procedures and guidelines
  • Roles, responsibilities and authority matrix
  • Previous findings and corrective actions
  • Applicable corporate, regulatory and EHSS requirements

Precious-Metals Population

  • Metal types, quantities and locations
  • Ownership and lifecycle status
  • Manufacturing units and processes involved
  • Items held onsite, in transit or with third parties

Procurement and Contracts

  • Purchase orders and procurement transactions
  • Purchase, lease, consignment or metal-account agreements
  • Ownership, liability, pricing and settlement terms
  • Recovery, refining and transportation contracts

Inventory and Custody

  • Receipts, issues, transfers and physical counts
  • Storage locations and access arrangements
  • Custody and chain-of-custody records
  • Inventory adjustments and discrepancy investigations

Accounting and Reconciliation

  • General-ledger and inventory balances
  • Valuation and accounting policies
  • Metal registers and reconciliations
  • Adjustments, provisions, settlements and write-offs

Recovery and Third Parties

  • Shipment, weighing, sampling and assay records
  • Recovery and refining statements
  • Third-party confirmations and balances
  • Settlement, losses and unresolved differences

Recommended review period: current year-to-date, the previous two financial years, and the latest complete recovery or settlement cycle where relevant.

Proposed Audit Approach

A risk-based sequence aligned with professional internal auditing practice.

Phase 1

Understand and Map

Map the lifecycle, functions, systems, records, third parties and transfers of responsibility or custody.

Phase 2

Assess Framework Design

Evaluate completeness of policies, responsibilities and controls; identify gaps, overlaps and fragmentation.

Phase 3

Test Implementation

Select risk-based samples and verify whether established controls are consistently and effectively applied.

Phase 4

Trace and Reconcile

Trace balances across procurement, custody, operations, third parties and financial records; investigate differences.

Phase 5

Review Third Parties

Assess contractual safeguards, confirmations, assay evidence, recovery records and settlement accountability.

Phase 6

Conclude and Report

Identify root causes, financial or control exposure, corrective actions, owners and realistic completion dates.

Audit focus
Precious metals remain the primary subject. Other materials or processes are considered only where necessary to assess ownership, custody, traceability, accounting, recovery or approved final disposition.
